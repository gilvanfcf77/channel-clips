metricas

metricas is a personal Google Cloud project, separate from the clips uploader. It is used by a script that runs on my own computer to read performance reports (views, watch time, engagement and estimated revenue) for the YouTube channels I own, so I can compare how my videos perform.

What it does

  • Reads analytics and basic video information for my own channels only.
  • Is read-only: it never uploads, edits or deletes anything on YouTube.
  • Saves the reports as local files on my computer.

Who uses it

Only me. It is not offered to the public, and no other person's account or data is accessed.
